＊ qtcreator_ja.qm から翻訳済みの recovered_ja.ts を生成
lconvert -i G:\qtcreator-18.0.1\share\qtcreator\translations\qtcreator_ja.qm -o recovered_ja.ts

＊ L10n のqtcreator_ja.ts へ recovered_ja.ts の翻訳内容を統合 (L10nは未翻訳部分が多いので)
lconvert -i qtcreator_ja.ts recovered_ja.ts -o qtcreator_ja_merged.ts

recovered_ja.ts を正規化 (<location line= などを削除)
lconvert -locations none -no-obsolete qtcreator_ja_merged.ts -o qtcreator_ja_norm.ts
